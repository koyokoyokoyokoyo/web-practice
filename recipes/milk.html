Ingredients

  • A source of milk (of your choice)

Steps

  1. pour milk into your drinking vessle of choice
  2. Place your drinking vessle of choice into the mcirowave
  3. Take drinking vessle out in 1 minute intervals to check on warmth
  4. Heat until desired warmth is reached
