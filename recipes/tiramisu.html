Tiramisu

Description

TIRAMISU IS A SOMETHING SOMETHING SOMETHING

THIS IS TIRAMISU PARAGRAPH 2

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • mascarpone(more than 500 gms)
  • vanilla extract
  • lady fingers
  • coffee of your choice
  • cocoa powder

Steps

  1. separate 5 eggs, and place the egg yolks in a bowl
  2. add 5 tablespoons of sugar
  3. mix until pale yellow and smooth
  4. add around 500gms of mascarpone
  5. a splash of vanilla extract
  6. stir until the mixture is creamy and fully combined
  7. Whip egg whites(with a electrical mixer) until stiff peaks (make sure it is really stiff ..)
  8. add mascarpone mixture to the bottom of a square pan
  9. add a row of lady fingers dipped in coffee
  10. add another layor of mascarpone on top of that
  11. repeat until you see fit.
  12. refrigerate overnight
  13. when ready to serve, dust generously with cocoa powder
